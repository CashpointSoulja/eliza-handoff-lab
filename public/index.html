Three policies, one story

The same 15 conversations, run through three versions of the handoff policy.

Baseline

v1, contain-first

It has the highest containment, but it quotes internal prices, promises rooms, transfers to empty rotas and misses safeguarding.

Passes gate

v2, handoff-aware

It checks the rota and the provider's rules, and routes safeguarding and clinical questions to named people. It passes every labelled case.

Blocked

v3, fast path

It is 700 ms faster, but its lighter safeguarding check misses the indirect disclosure in S04. The gate blocks it.
